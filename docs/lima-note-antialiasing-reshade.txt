33lima, there are two new things on the graphics side you might want to
try, and both are switched off unless you ask for them.

The setup has a new entry, number 13, called visual enhancements. It
turns on four times antialiasing, sixteen times anisotropic filtering
and ReShade all in one go, and pressing it a second time takes the lot
back off again. The antialiasing is the one that cleans up the jagged
edges on wings and along the horizon. The filtering is what stops the
ground going to mush when you are looking along it at a shallow angle,
which from a cockpit is most of what you see.

A word of warning given your 770. All three together are real work for
that card, and ReShade adds five passes on top of every single frame. If
the frame rate suffers I would put the antialiasing on by itself first,
which is item 6 in the dgVoodoo settings, and leave ReShade alone until
you know what the rest of it is costing you. Four times is the setting I
would stay with. Eight was tried here and it put visible seams along the
edges of the terrain tiles, as well as costing more for no real gain.

If you do run ReShade, the DEL key opens its overlay while you are
flying, and you can switch the individual effects on and off in there to
see what each one is actually giving you rather than judging the whole
lot at once. Page Up and Page Down move between the five presets that
ship with it, from Subtle at one end to Showcase at the other, so you
can compare them in the same scene instead of guessing.

None of it turns itself on, and if you decide against ReShade later you
can switch it off without losing your presets.
